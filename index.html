How the game works

Players: 2+ · Platform: one phone, passed around.

  1. A random prompt appears (e.g. “Things you see in a supermarket”).
  2. Each player says a new valid answer and passes the phone.
  3. A secret timer runs; whoever holds the phone when it goes “boom” loses the round.

Designed for face-to-face play: quick thinking, verbal creativity and less solo screen time.
